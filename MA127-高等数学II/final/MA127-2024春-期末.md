## 一

Multiple Choice Questions: (only one correct answer for each of the following questions.)

### 1-1

Suppose that both partial derivatives $f_{x}$ and $f_{y}$ of $f$ exist at $P_{0}(x_{0}, y_{0})$. Which of the following must be correct?

(A) $f$ is continuous at $P_{0}$.

(B) $f$ is not necessarily continuous at $P_{0}$, but $\lim\limits_{(x, y) \rightarrow(x_{0}, y_{0})} f(x, y)$ exists.

(C) All the directional derivatives of $f$ at $P_{0}$ exist.

(D) None of the above statements is correct.

### 1-2

The gradient of the function $f(x, y)=\arctan \frac{x}{y}$ at the point $(0,1)$ is

(A) $i$.

(B) $-i$.

(C) $j$.

(D) $-j$.

### 1-3

If $f(x, y)$ is differentiable at $(0,0)$ and $\nabla f(0,0)=2 i+j$, then which one of the following values is impossible to be a directional derivative of $f(x, y)$ at $(0,0)$ ?

(A) $\sqrt{5}$.

(B) $-\sqrt{5}$.

(C) $\sqrt{5}-1$.

(D) $-\sqrt{5}-1$.

### 1-4

Let $f(x)=x \sin x \cos x$. Then $f^{(100)}(0)=$

(A) $\frac{2^{98}}{99!}$.

(B) $-\frac{2^{98}}{99!}$.

(C) $100 \cdot 2^{98}$.

(D) $-100 \cdot 2^{98}$.

### 1-5

Which of the following statements must be correct?

(A) If $a_{n}>0$ and $\lim\limits_{n \rightarrow \infty} n^{2}\left(e^{\frac{1}{n}}-1\right) a_{n}=2$, then $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{n}$ converges.

(B) If $a_{n}>0$ and $\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_{n}}<1(n=1,2, \cdots)$, then $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{n}$ converges.

(C) If $\lim\limits_{n \rightarrow \infty} a_{n} b_{n}=1$, then at least one of the series $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{n}$ or $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty} b_{n}$ diverges.

(D) If $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{n}$ converges, then $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{n} a_{n+1}$ converges.
